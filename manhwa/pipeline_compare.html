⚠️ Known Gaps in Episode 2
Age Timeline not updated
If characters age or change appearance in Ep2, the phase tracker won't know. Fix: run Re-plan Part after manually adding phases in the Entity Editor.
New entity ref images missing
If Ep2 introduces a new character or location, they won't have a ref image (IP-Adapter anchor). Fix: go to Entity Editor → select new entity → regenerate ref.
Stale beat plans (fixed by Re-plan)
Plans computed before our fixes have bad action text + empty characters. Hit 🔄 Re-plan Part to clear and recompute cleanly.
Ep1 batch planning also parallel
Ep1's parallel batching means location doesn't chain between Ep1 batches either — only Ep2 benefits from sequential chaining so far.
